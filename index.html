The AP Calculus Time Capsule

We are finally done with Calculus, Summer is just around the corner. You're sitting in calculus class doing absolutely nothing productive when suddenly Ms. Drum walks in carrying the giant bin of missing calculators. She should be relaxed. She should be letting everyone chill. But instead, she drops the bin on the desk and says, 'WHERE ARE MY CALCULATORS??'

The class barely reacts. Adit is in the back of the room with his phone watching TikToks at full volume and rage baiting everyone around him while Allen argues with him across the classroom. Shaurya is just being nonchalant in the corner, Abhinav is half asleep, and Arnav is struggling to figure out the wordle.

You lean back in your chair when the whiteboard suddenly glitches. A glowing sine graph tears across the board and starts pulling papers, desks, and backpacks toward it while old quizzes, which we all failed, from September fly through the air.

That's when you realize this isn't normal.
The room begins shaking.
And now...

Adit looks around and goes, 'Wait... where's my invite?'

Room 1: The Limit & Derivatives of Time

We are now in Ms. Drum's memories of AP Calculus.

You wake up back in September. The classroom looks older, unfinished notes cover the walls, and everyone is suddenly holding the very first limits quiz. Every paper has the same giant red number written across the top.

67.

Ms. Drum slowly walks to the front of the room holding the grade spreadsheet while the class completely loses it. Someone yells '67!' and suddenly everybody joins in.

'67! 67! 67!'

The chanting echoes through the room while Adit screams 'Big Boy Yalla,' Shaurya argues the quiz was unfair, and Arnav is already calculating how much the grade destroyed his average.

Then the lights flicker.

The board suddenly fills itself with impossible limit problems while giant glowing 67s flash across the walls. A lock appears on the classroom door with five empty spaces beneath it.

That's when you realize the only way to escape the Limits & Derivatives Era is to solve the problems that started it all.
